# AIVEXUS Score — draft privacy information (review before any release)

AIVEXUS Score v0.1 has no account registration, advertisements, trackers, analytics,
cloud inference, paid features or external API calls. Its scores and participant
names are stored locally on the device/browser using the Flutter
shared_preferences plugin. These are accessible to anyone who can access that
device/browser profile and may be removed when app data/browser storage is erased.

The app does not intentionally transmit participant names or scores to AIVEXUS.
Distribution marketplaces (e.g. itch.io or Google Play), payment providers, and
hosting services operate independently and may process their own account, payment,
download, security or analytics data under their own notices.

This is NOT a final GDPR privacy policy. Prior to publishing, the owner must verify
the distributed binary's manifest/network behavior, identify the legal publisher and
contact details, determine whether any platform/analytics services collect data,
and complete the marketplace's privacy and data-safety declarations. Terms, payment,
consumer rights and French/EU compliance require separate verification.

No published commercial distribution is authorized by this file.
